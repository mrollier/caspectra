**Is 18 pp acceptable?** Yes. Chaos imposes no hard cap on regular articles; the main text is ~11 pp with the rest in appendices that earn their place (registration record, algorithms, prior-work table). The growth from 15 pp is entirely the referee-required figures plus the appendix restructure, not new claims.

**Strengths worth recording at this stage** (balance): (S1) the traceability discipline — every prior-round mandate is locatable in a named commit with a moved-material ledger, which made this verification unusually cheap; (S2) Fig. 1 and Fig. 5 are model examples of pedagogy without overclaim (Fig. 5 shows only the three rules where two independent criteria agree, and says so); (S3) the R4 execution goes beyond the request, putting the bound in the abstract itself; (S4) methodological hygiene (pre-registration revs. 2–14, exchangeability framing, ρ metric) remains the paper's spine and survived the rewrite untouched.
